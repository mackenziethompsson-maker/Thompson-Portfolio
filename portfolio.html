Project Context

The project investigated how phase change materials (PCMs) can be used as temporary thermal buffers to absorb transient heat loads in cooling systems. The broader goal was to develop a validated analytical modelling approach capable of reducing dependence on lengthy experimental testing.

Problem

Conventional thermal management systems are typically designed around steady-state operating conditions, making them inefficient for applications dominated by short-duration thermal spikes. Experimental optimisation of these systems is often slow and resource-intensive, requiring repeated physical testing for every design iteration.

Applications & Future Relevance

PCM thermal buffering systems have strong potential in aerospace and high-performance computing applications where transient thermal loads and strict mass constraints limit conventional cooling. This technology could support future satellite electronics and space-based data centres by providing lightweight, passive thermal management capable of handling short-duration heat spikes efficiently.

Objective

Develop an analytical MATLAB model capable of predicting the thermal behaviour of a PCM-based latent thermal energy storage system and validate the model against experimental data.

The system was designed to optimise inlet vs outlet temperatures and provide a framework for accelerating future thermal design optimisation.

Methodology

The project combined analytical MATLAB modelling and experimental validation to investigate the thermal behaviour of a PCM-based latent energy storage system. Simulation results were compared directly against physical testing to evaluate system performance, improve model accuracy, and reduce reliance on repeated experimental iteration.

My Contribution

Led the development of the project's analytical MATLAB modelling framework, including transient energy balance calculations, PCM melting analysis, and outlet temperature prediction methods.

Collaborated with masters students to establish a comparison of analytical predictions against experimental results to improve model reliability and support future optimisation work.

Project 02

Is Biomass Carbon Neutral?

This project investigated whether agricultural biomass can be a truly sustainable and carbon-neutral alternative to fossil fuels. The research examined the full process of biomass energy production, including how agricultural waste is collected, converted into energy, and managed within existing environmental and policy frameworks. It also explored the opportunities and challenges associated with using biomass as part of the global transition to cleaner energy sources.

Key Findings

Biomass is not inherently carbon neutral, but it can become a low-carbon energy source when managed sustainably. Environmental benefits depend on responsible sourcing, effective emissions management, and clear carbon accounting practices.

My Contribution

Contributed to the investigation of arguments both supporting and challenging biomass carbon neutrality. Analysed three international case studies focused on sugarcane residue, agricultural waste, and bagasse ethanol systems in Brazil, New South Wales, and Bolivia to evaluate their environmental and sustainability outcomes.

Project 01 · Client: UTS Rocketry

Static Rocket Fire Stand

UTS Rocketry were seeking a test stand to measure and validate rocket motors prior to launch. There were four main components: the adjustable frame which can be fixed to the ground, a load cell for measuring thrust force, an ignition system, and software to record and visualise the thrust curve on the tested motor.

This was a highly client-focused project with strict design requirements gathered directly from UTS Rocketry. Throughout the project, continuous collaboration with the key stakeholder was necessary to ensure satisfactory completion.

Outcomes

The final test stand successfully passed all validation testing and met all design requirements. The system demonstrated reliable thrust measurement and data acquisition capabilities during a successful test fire conducted prior to project handover, delivering a functional solution that supports safer rocket motor validation before launch operations.

My Contribution

Led the procurement process, managing documentation and supplier coordination to minimise costs and maximise the available budget. Served as lead assembly operator during system integration and testing. Contributed to CAD design and manufacturing of key structural and mechanical components.

Project 02

Solar BBQ

★ 2nd Highest Temperature in Cohort

The goal of this project was to design, develop, and prototype a solar-powered, portable BBQ that converts solar energy into heat for cooking. The initial concept was a parabolic dish-style design that focused sunlight into a central cooking zone. Project 03 · Product Pitch

Klip-Board — Modular Electric Skateboard

Klip-Board was developed as a modular electric skateboard system aimed at improving accessibility and convenience for university students and urban commuters. The project focused on designing a clip-on electric skateboard truck capable of converting almost any standard skateboard into an electric skateboard through a simple, non-invasive installation process.

A major design priority was preserving the authentic skateboarding experience by maintaining traditional steering characteristics and allowing full customisation of decks and wheels. Key design considerations included portability, ease of installation, rider safety, and user flexibility — leading to features such as regenerative braking and quick-attach mechanisms. Through market research, feasibility analysis, and engineering evaluation, the final concept demonstrated strong potential as a practical, user-focused personal mobility solution.

My Contribution

Contributed as Lead CAD Engineer, responsible for detailed modelling and integration of the modular truck assembly and mounting system. Assisted stakeholder consultation to identify user expectations, commuting pain points, and desired functionality — guiding iterative design improvements. Also led revenue and feasibility estimation activities, supporting the evaluation of manufacturing practicality, scalability, and overall project viability.
